Comprehensive Oily Body Conditions Guide

1. Body Acne (Bacne & Chest Acne)

Symptoms: Red inflammatory bumps, pus-filled pimples, blackheads, and painful breakout clusters across the back, chest, and shoulders caused by excess sebum and trapped dead skin cells.

[ Image Space: Body Acne Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Methods: Chemical exfoliation with Salicylic Acid (BHA) to deep-clean inside pores, targeted spot treatments, and showering immediately after sweating or exercising.

Cure Products: Oxecure Body Acne Spray, Neutrogena Body Clear Body Wash, or Cosrx Salicylic Acid Daily Gentle Cleanser.

[ Image Space: Oxecure / Neutrogena Cure Products ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Wash skin daily with non-comedogenic cleansers, change bed sheets regularly, avoid tight synthetic clothing, and use oil-free light moisturizers.

Protection Products: CeraVe SA Smoothing Cleanser, Cetaphil Oily Skin Cleanser, or Simple Kind to Skin Refreshing Shower Gel.

[ Image Space: CeraVe / Cetaphil Protection Products ]
2. Fungal Folliculitis (Pityrosporum Folliculitis)

Symptoms: Batches of uniform, small, highly itchy red bumps or tiny pustules around hair follicles on the chest, back, and upper arms, triggered when excess oil feeds Malassezia yeast.

[ Image Space: Fungal Folliculitis Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Methods: Washing affected areas with anti-fungal treatments (leaving them on skin for 3–5 minutes before rinsing), keeping skin dry, and avoiding heavy occlusive creams.

Cure Products: Nizoral Shampoo / Wash (Ketoconazole), Benzac AC Wash (Benzoyl Peroxide), or Oxecure Sulfur Soap.

[ Image Space: Nizoral / Benzac Cure Products ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Wear breathable, loose cotton clothing in humid weather, thoroughly dry skin after bathing, and refrain from applying raw oils to oily body areas.

Protection Products: Mentholatum Acnes Body Wash, Bioderma Sébium Gel Moussant, or Nivea Fresh & Bright Gel Lotion.

[ Image Space: Mentholatum / Bioderma Protection Products ]
3. Keratosis Pilaris (Aggravated by Clogged Pores)

Symptoms: Rough, bumpy "goosebump-like" texture on the upper arms, thighs, or buttocks that becomes inflamed and reddish when excess oil and keratin plug hair follicles.

[ Image Space: Keratosis Pilaris Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Methods: Combining Beta Hydroxy Acids (BHA) to dissolve oil plugs with Alpha Hydroxy Acids (AHA) or Urea to smooth surface texture without physical scrubbing.

Cure Products: CeraVe SA Smoothing Lotion, Paula's Choice 2% BHA Body Spot Exfoliant, or The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution.

[ Image Space: CeraVe SA / Paula's Choice Cure Products ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Avoid harsh physical scrubs or loofahs that irritate hair follicles, moisturize daily with oil-free chemical exfoliants, and use mild soap-free body washes.

Protection Products: Avene Cleanance Cleansing Gel, Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser, or Sebamed Liquid Face & Body Wash.

[ Image Space: Avene / Sebamed Protection Products ]
4. Seborrheic Dermatitis (Body & Chest Scaling)

Symptoms: Red, oily patches covered with yellowish or greasy scales accompanied by mild itching, most common on the sternum (center of chest), upper back, and skin folds.

[ Image Space: Seborrheic Dermatitis Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Methods: Regulating yeast growth on oily areas using anti-fungal wash solutions, avoiding hot water showers, and applying lightweight soothing gel treatments.

Cure Products: Selsun Blue Suspension (Selenium Sulfide), Nizoral Shampoo, or Sebamed Clear Face Care Gel.

[ Image Space: Selsun Blue / Sebamed Cure Products ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Maintain gentle daily skin hygiene, avoid thick body butter or heavy mineral oils on greasy patches, and wear moisture-wicking clothing.

Protection Products: La Roche-Posay Effaclar Purifying Foaming Gel, Cosrx Low pH Good Morning Gel Cleanser, or Avene Cleanance Cleansing Gel.

[ Image Space: La Roche-Posay / Cosrx Protection Products ]
5. Hydradenitis Suppurativa / Intertrigo (Friction & Sebum Inflammations)

Symptoms: Red, inflamed, painful bumps or chafed rash in areas where skin rubs against skin (under arms, under breasts, inner thighs) where excess oil and sweat get trapped.

[ Image Space: Intertrigo Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Methods: Applying antibacterial washes, keeping folds clean and completely dry, using zinc-based protective barrier creams, and managing friction.

Cure Products: Betadine Skin Cleanser, Dettol Antiseptic Wash, or Sudocrem Healing Cream.

[ Image Space: Betadine / Sudocrem Cure Products ]

Protection Products: Snake Brand Prickly Heat Powder, Vaseline Healthy Bright Fresh & Radiant Gel, or CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser.

[ Image Space: Snake Brand / Vaseline Protection Products ]

[ Image Space: Eucerin Itch Relief / Aloe Gel ]
