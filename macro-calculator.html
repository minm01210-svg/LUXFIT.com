Macro Calculator

Fuel Your Goals

Macros — protein, carbs, and fats — are the building blocks of every meal plan. Getting them right helps you lose fat, build muscle, or maintain performance.

How It Works

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate your BMR, multiply by your activity level for TDEE, then split macros based on your goal.

Protein supports muscle repair. Carbs fuel training. Fats support hormones and recovery.
