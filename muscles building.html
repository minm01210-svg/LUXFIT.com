Tips for Building Muscles

  • Focus on compound exercises: such as squats, deadlifts, bench presses, and pull-ups. These exercises engage multiple muscle groups and promote overall strength and muscle growth.

  • Progressive overload: Gradually increase the weight, reps, or intensity of your workouts over time. This challenges your muscles and stimulates growth.
