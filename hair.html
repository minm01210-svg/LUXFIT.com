Comprehensive Sensitive Hair & Scalp Conditions Guide

1. Contact Dermatitis (Allergic or Irritant)

Symptoms: Localized redness, intense itching, burning, tingling sensations, small red bumps, or mild swelling along the hairline and scalp.

[ Image Space: Contact Dermatitis Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Immediately stop using the offending hair product and calm the active inflammation.

Cure Products: Pure Aloe Vera Gel (e.g., Nature Republic Aloe Vera 92% Soothing Gel) or a mild topical hydrocortisone cream from a local pharmacy for severe itching.

[ Image Space: Contact Dermatitis Cure Product ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Perform a patch test on your arm before using new products, and stick to hypoallergenic, mild formulas.

Protection Products: Sebamed Everyday Shampoo (pH 5.5) or gentle baby shampoos (e.g., Johnson's Baby Shampoo, widely available across Myanmar).

[ Image Space: Contact Dermatitis Protection Product ]
2. Scalp Hyperesthesia (Scalp Sensitivity / Trichodynia)

Symptoms: A burning, prickling, or "hair aching" sensation when touching, moving, or brushing the hair, often without visible redness.

[ Image Space: Scalp Hyperesthesia Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Relieve mechanical stress from the hair follicles and apply cooling, nerve-calming scalp treatments.

Cure Products: Lightweight scalp tonics containing Centella Asiatica or Panthenol (e.g., Some By Mi Cica Peptide Anti Hair Loss Derma Scalp Tonic).

[ Image Space: Scalp Hyperesthesia Cure Product ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Keep hair tied loosely, switch to gentle hair ties, and use soft-bristled combs to prevent pulling.

Protection Products: Soft silk/satin scrunchies and wide-tooth wooden or detangling combs (available at local accessory shops, MINISO, or online).

[ Image Space: Scalp Hyperesthesia Protection Product ]
3. Environmental & Weather-Induced Flare-Ups

Symptoms: Sudden tightness, mild itching, surface prickling, and increased scalp tenderness when stepping into extreme weather.

[ Image Space: Weather-Induced Flare-Ups Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Wash away trapped pollutants with gentle cleansers and restore hydration to the scalp skin barrier.

Cure Products: Lightweight hydrating serums containing Hyaluronic Acid or Ceramides (e.g., L'Oréal Paris Elseve Hyaluron Moisture Serum).

[ Image Space: Weather-Induced Flare-Ups Cure Product ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Cover your hair when stepping out into direct sunlight or heavy pollution, and wash with lukewarm water instead of hot water.

Protection Products: Wide-brimmed UV sun hats, UV-blocking umbrellas, and leave-in hair mists with UV protection.

[ Image Space: Weather-Induced Flare-Ups Protection Product ]
4. Mild Scalp Folliculitis

Symptoms: Small, red, tender bumps or tiny whitehead pimples around the base of hair follicles that itch or feel sore to the touch.

[ Image Space: Mild Scalp Folliculitis Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Use mild antibacterial or antimicrobial cleansers to clear out blocked pores without stripping natural moisture.

Cure Products: Short-term antifungal/antioxidant scalp washes (e.g., Selsun Anti-Dandruff Shampoo used 1–2 times a week, or Tea Tree Scalp Serums).

[ Image Space: Mild Scalp Folliculitis Cure Product ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Avoid putting thick hair oils or leave-in conditioners directly on the scalp root, and wash hair promptly after heavy sweating.

Protection Products: Gentle silicone scalp massager brushes (for light exfoliation while washing) and lightweight, water-based scalp sprays.

[ Image Space: Mild Scalp Folliculitis Protection Product ]
5. Sensitive-Scalp Flaking (Non-Oily Dandruff)

Symptoms: Fine, small, dry white flakes that fall easily, accompanied by scalp tightness, dryness, and persistent itching.

[ Image Space: Sensitive Flaking Symptoms ]
💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Use non-drying active anti-flake formulas that soothe the scalp skin barrier while clearing away flakes.

Cure Products: Hydrating, anti-dandruff shampoos containing mild active ingredients (e.g., Head & Shoulders Supreme Series or Sebamed Anti-Dandruff Shampoo).

[ Image Space: Sensitive Flaking Cure Product ]
🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Methods: Wash hair 2–3 times a week instead of daily, avoid high-heat blow drying directly on the roots, and use pH-balanced products.

Protection Products: Balanced daily cleansers like Sebamed Everyday Shampoo (pH 5.5) and leave-in moisturizing mists.

[ Image Space: Sensitive Flaking Protection Product ]

Cause: Excess oil and hair products migrating down from the scalp and hairline, clogging the pores along the forehead and temples.
