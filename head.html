Head & Face Care

Comprehensive Sensitive Face Conditions Guide

1. Erythema & Persistent Facial Redness

Symptoms: Flush/red spots across cheeks, chin, or nose, heightened warmth on skin, and sensitivity to thermal changes or hot beverages.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Soothe micro-inflammation using concentrated Centella Asiatica (Cica), Niacinamide, and Panthenol to quickly reduce localized redness.

Cure Products: SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule or Dear, Klairs Rich Moist Soothing Serum.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Shield skin against climate stress and UV exposure using mineral-based sunscreens enriched with anti-redness calming agents.

Protection Products: Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun SPF50+ or La Roche-Posay Anthelios Mineral Sunscreen.

2. Facial Contact Dermatitis & Allergic Flare-ups

Symptoms: Itchy red patches, micro-swelling, tiny hives, or localized scaling caused by active cosmetics, synthetic fragrances, or essential oils.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Immediately stop all active skincare products and calm cutaneous irritation using soothing barrier gels or hydrocortisone lotion if severe.

Cure Products: Nature Republic Aloe Vera 92% Soothing Gel or La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Baume B5+.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Simplify skincare routines down to 3 hypoallergenic essential products and always perform a 24-hour patch test behind the ear before full face application.

Protection Products: Bioderma Sensibio H2O Micellar Water and Simple Kind to Skin Hydrating Cleanser.

3. Compromised Sensitive Skin Barrier

Symptoms: Extreme skin tightness, raw sensitivity upon touch, persistent dehydration, and burning when applying basic water-based products.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Rebuild missing skin lipid layers with lipid-identical ceramides, free fatty acids, and cholesterol complexes.

Cure Products: CeraVe Moisturizing Cream or KraveBeauty Great Barrier Relief.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Avoid hot water rinses, physical scrubs, and strong chemical exfoliants (AHAs/BHAs) to maintain lipid barrier integrity.

Protection Products: Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser or Physiogel Daily Moisture Therapy Cream.

Comprehensive Dry Face Conditions Guide

1. Xerosis Cutis (Extreme Facial Dryness & Tightness)

Symptoms: Persistent feeling of tightness after washing, dull appearance, fine surface lines, and uncomfortable skin stretching when smiling or expressing.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Replenish lost moisture deep within the stratum corneum using humectants like Hyaluronic Acid or snail mucin, followed by emollient creams to seal hydration in place.

Cure Products (Available in MM): COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence or The Ordinary Hyaluronic Acid 2% + B5.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Avoid hot water rinses and harsh sulfate cleansers that strip natural oils. Use ultra-gentle, non-foaming hydrating cleansers daily.

Protection Products (Available in MM): Cetaphil Gentle Skin Cleanser or CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser.

2. Flaking & Compromised Skin Barrier

Symptoms: Visible skin peeling/flaking around the nose and mouth, rough texture, stinging or burning when applying standard products, and patchy makeup application.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Pause all active chemical exfoliants (AHAs/BHAs) and retinoids. Rebuild the lipid barrier using concentrated ceramide, fatty acid, and peptide complexes.

Cure Products (Available in MM): CeraVe Moisturizing Cream or Ezerra Cream.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Lock in moisture at night using occlusive barrier creams, and wear physical/mineral sunscreen daily to prevent UV rays from further degrading the damaged barrier.

Protection Products (Available in MM): Cetaphil Optimal Hydration Daily Cream and Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics SPF50+.

3. Facial Eczema / Atopic Dryness

Symptoms: Red, itchy patches, rough micro-cracks on the cheeks or around the eyes, heightened skin reactivity, and seasonal flare-ups during dry/air-conditioned weather.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Apply soothing anti-inflammatory plant extracts like Centella Asiatica (Cica) and Panthenol to reduce redness and curb the itch cycle.

Cure Products (Available in MM): SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule or La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Baume B5+.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Keep indoor sleeping spaces humidified and use fragrance-free, hypoallergenic moisturizers free of essential oils and artificial perfumes.

Protection Products (Available in MM): Physiogel Daily Moisture Therapy Cream or Cetaphil Moisturising Cream.

4. Dehydrated Surface with Fine Dry Lines

Symptoms: Skin looks oily on top (rebound oiliness) yet feels parched underneath; fine criss-cross dehydrating lines appear on the cheeks when pushed upward.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Layer oil-free water gels and hydration mists that quickly penetrate without creating a heavy, greasy film.

Cure Products (Available in MM): Neutrogena Hydro Boost Water Gel / Gel-Cream or Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Cream.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Apply lightweight hydrating sunscreens consistently and mist with soothing thermal water throughout the day in air-conditioned environments.

Protection Products (Available in MM): SKIN1004 Hyalu-Cica Water-Fit Sun Serum SPF50+.

Comprehensive Oily Face & Acne Conditions Guide

1. Excess Sebum & Congested Pores

Symptoms: Persistent shine across the T-zone, enlarged pores, non-inflammatory blackheads, and uneven skin texture.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Use Beta Hydroxy Acids (Salicylic Acid) to penetrate deep into pores and break down hardened sebum plugs.

Cure Products: Paula's Choice 2% BHA Liquid Exfoliant or Cosrx BHA Blackhead Power Liquid.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Regulate oil production daily using Niacinamide and hydrate with oil-free gel formulas.

Protection Products: The Ordinary Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1% & Neutrogena Hydro Boost Water Gel.

2. Inflammatory Acne (Papules, Pustules, Nodules & Cysts)

Symptoms: Tender red bumps, pus-filled pimples, localized swelling, and heightened skin sensitivity.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Spot-treat with Benzoyl Peroxide or Azelaic Acid to neutralize acne-causing bacteria and reduce redness.

Cure Products: CeraVe Acne Foaming Cream Cleanser (4% Benzoyl Peroxide) or Paula's Choice 10% Azelaic Acid Booster.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Shield inflamed skin with lightweight, fluid SPF daily to prevent post-acne hyperpigmentation.

Protection Products: La Roche-Posay Anthelios UVMune 400 Oil Control Fluid SPF 50+.

3. Subclinical Acne & Closed Comedones

Symptoms: Tiny, skin-colored bumps clustered along the forehead or jawline that rarely turn into red pimples.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Accelerate cellular turnover using topical retinoids or Alpha Hydroxy Acid (AHA) blends to loosen trapped dead skin.

Cure Products: Differin Gel (Adapalene 0.1%) or Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Avoid heavy comedogenic oils or wax-based cosmetics that seal the skin surface.

Protection Products: CeraVe PM Facial Moisturizing Lotion (Oil-Free, Non-Comedogenic).

4. Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (Dark Spots)

Symptoms: Dark brown or red discoloration marks left behind after acne blemishes heal.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Inhibit melanin overproduction using targeted brightening actives like Alpha Arbutin, Tranexamic Acid, or Vitamin C.

Cure Products: SkinCeuticals Silymarin CF or The Ordinary Alpha Arbutin 2% + HA.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen consistently; UV exposure darkens active post-acne marks significantly.

Protection Products: Anessa Perfect UV Sunscreen Skincare Gel SPF 50+.

5. Dehydrated Oily Skin Barrier

Symptoms: Skin produces heavy surface oil while feeling tight, flaky, or uncomfortable underneath due to moisture loss.

💊 CURE & TREATMENT METHOD

Cure Method: Temporarily pause active exfoliants and repair the skin barrier using ceramide-rich water formulations.

Cure Products: KraveBeauty Great Barrier Relief or COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence.

🛡️ PROTECTION & PREVENTION METHOD

Protection Method: Wash with pH-balanced, non-stripping cleansers instead of harsh sulfate soaps that trigger rebound oiliness.

Protection Products: Cetaphil Oily Skin Cleanser or Youth To The People Superfood Cleanser.
